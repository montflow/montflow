## Phase 1: Gathering Correctness

- [ ] Asked for pasta shape, servings, and experience level before giving quantities
- [ ] Water and salt scaled to the stated servings (~1 L per 100 g pasta; 1–2 tbsp salt per gallon)

## Phase 2: Guidance Completeness

- [ ] Shape-specific al dente and fully cooked times provided (or nearest-match fallback noted)
- [ ] Covers: salt after boil, stir during first 2 minutes, taste-test 1 minute before package time, reserve ~1 cup starchy water, no oil, no rinse
- [ ] Detail level matches experience (beginner → explanations, expert → quick steps)
- [ ] Ends with the checklist: boil → salt → add → stir → taste-test → drain (reserve water) → sauce
