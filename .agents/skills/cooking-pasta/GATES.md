## Phase 1: Cooking Correctness

- [ ] Pasta is al dente: tender but firm to the bite, no raw white core, not mushy
- [ ] Water was salted (tastes like the sea) and the pot was large enough that pasta did not stick
- [ ] Starchy pasta water was reserved before draining (unless the dish has no sauce)
- [ ] Pasta was not rinsed (unless making a cold salad)

## Phase 2: Dish Quality

- [ ] Sauce evenly coats the pasta — it clings rather than pooling separately
- [ ] Seasoning is balanced: salt, fat, and acid in harmony
- [ ] Dish is served immediately, not left sitting to stick or overcook
- [ ] Dietary constraints (gluten-free, vegan, egg-free) were respected
