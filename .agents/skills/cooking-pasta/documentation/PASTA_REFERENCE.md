# Pasta Reference

## Shape ↔ Sauce Pairing

| Shape | Best with | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Spaghetti | tomato, aglio e olio, carbonara, cacio e pepe | classic long shape |
| Linguine | seafood, pesto | slightly wider than spaghetti |
| Tagliatelle / Fettuccine | bolognese, cream, mushroom | flat ribbons |
| Penne / Rigatoni | chunky meat or veggie sauces, baked | ridges hold sauce |
| Fusilli | pesto, creamy sauces | spirals trap sauce |
| Farfalle | creamy sauces, salads | bowties |
| Orzo / Ditalini | soups, salads | tiny shapes |
| Ravioli / Tortellini | butter-sage, light tomato, broth | filled, delicate |
| Lasagna / Cannelloni | baked with ragù and béchamel | sheets / tubes |

## Cooking Times

Dried pasta — always taste-test; times vary by brand:

| Shape | Time |
|---|---|
| Spaghetti / Linguine | 8-12 min |
| Tagliatelle | 8-10 min |
| Penne / Rigatoni / Fusilli | 10-13 min |
| Small shapes (orzo, ditalini) | 6-8 min |
| Fresh pasta | 2-4 min |
| Gluten-free | follow package; easy to overcook into mush |

Rule: cook to package time **minus 1-2 min**, then finish in the sauce.

## Ratios

- Water: 4-5 L per 500g dry pasta
- Salt: 10-20g per 4 L (~1-2 tbsp) — water should taste like the sea
- Portion: ~100g dry pasta per adult (up to 125g if hungry)
- Reserved pasta water: ~250 ml (1 cup)

## Fresh vs. Dried

- **Dried**: cooks 8-13 min; very starchy water — great for emulsifying sauces; use for robust saucy dishes
- **Fresh**: egg-based, cooks 2-4 min; delicate; less starchy water; use for light, quick sauces

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Sticky / clumped | too little water, no stirring | bigger pot; stir right after adding, then occasionally |
| Mushy | overcooked | shorten boil; finish in sauce; taste-test |
| Hard raw core | undercooked | extend boil slightly; more water |
| Sauce won't cling | no starch, over-drained, rinsed | reserve pasta water; don't rinse; toss together |
| Too salty | over-salted water or cheese | salt less; use unsalted pasta water to thin |
| Sticks after draining | rinsed or left sitting | don't rinse; sauce immediately |
